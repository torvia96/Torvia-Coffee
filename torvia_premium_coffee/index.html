GOOD TO KNOW

Is delivery included in the price?

No. The amount shown in your cart covers the products only. Delivery charges depend on your PIN code and order weight, and we confirm them with you on WhatsApp before you pay anything.

How do I pay?

Once you send your order on WhatsApp, we reply with the final bill including delivery and share UPI or bank transfer details. Nothing is charged on this website.

Can I order more than one product?

Yes — add as many as you like. Your cart keeps everything together and sends one combined order.
